# Responding to suspicious sign-in reports

Treat a report of unfamiliar sign-ins or possible account compromise as a security case. Confirm the affected account through the approved verification procedure and collect approximate times, locations as shown to the user, and safe alert details. Do not ask for passwords, MFA codes, or session tokens.

## Protect the account

Do not disclose account information to an unverified requester or perform an account change outside the approved security process. If the verified user still has access, direct them to the product's documented security controls and credential-rotation steps. Do not delete audit evidence or dismiss the report based only on an unfamiliar location.

## Escalate

Route the case to the security team promptly, noting whether the user recognizes the activity and whether access is currently blocked. Share only necessary evidence through approved channels. The security team determines containment and follow-up; support must not claim that an account is safe until that review is complete.
