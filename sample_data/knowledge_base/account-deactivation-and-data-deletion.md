# Account deactivation and data deletion requests

Treat requests to close an account, erase workspace data, or exercise a privacy right as consequential. Confirm the requester's identity using the approved verification procedure, identify the account and affected workspace, and clarify the requested scope without promising immediate deletion.

## Preserve the request and escalate

Record the request and route it to the designated privacy or account team. Do not delete data, disable an account, or instruct another customer to perform deletion as a workaround. The responsible team must confirm applicable retention requirements, dependencies, and completion before a customer is told the request is fulfilled.

## Keep communications factual

Acknowledge the request, explain that it has been referred for review, and avoid inventing a completion date or legal conclusion. Include only information necessary to process the request and do not copy unrelated customer data into the case.
