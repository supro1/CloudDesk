# Troubleshooting external file sharing

Confirm which file or folder is being shared, the user's role in the workspace, and the exact message shown. Do not ask the customer to make sensitive content public as a diagnostic step.

## Check workspace policy

An administrator may restrict external sharing for the entire workspace or for particular content. Ask an authorized administrator to review the applicable sharing controls and the recipient domain. Confirm that the customer is allowed to share the content with that recipient before suggesting any policy change.

## Check the link and recipient

Verify that the link has not expired or been revoked and that the recipient is using the intended account. If access is limited to named people, the recipient may need to sign in with the invited email address. If the settings appear correct, collect the time, workspace, and redacted error details and escalate.
