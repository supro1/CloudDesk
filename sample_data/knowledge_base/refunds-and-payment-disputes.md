# Handling refund requests and payment disputes

Confirm the account, transaction date, amount, and the customer's description using authorized billing records. Do not request or store full card numbers, security codes, or banking passwords. Never promise that a refund will be issued before the request is reviewed.

## Refund requests

Summarize the customer's reason and the relevant invoice details, then submit the request through the configured approval workflow. A refund is not complete unless an authorized person approves it and the approved action is recorded. Do not submit a duplicate request while an existing request is under review.

## Payment disputes

If the customer says they have opened or intend to open a charge dispute, preserve the relevant case details and escalate to the billing team. Do not attempt to reverse, contest, or alter a disputed charge through an unapproved action. Keep the response factual and avoid guaranteeing an outcome or timeline.
