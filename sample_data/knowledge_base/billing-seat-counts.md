# Understanding seat counts and subscription charges

When a customer questions a seat charge, compare the invoice period with the subscription's seat count and the workspace's member history for that same period. A current member count may differ from the quantity billed earlier in the billing cycle.

## Investigate the difference

Confirm the customer, subscription, invoice date, billed quantity, and the reported active-seat count. Check whether invitations, pending members, or recent membership changes are included in the product's seat accounting. Explain the specific records that support the comparison without exposing another customer's information.

## Corrections and credits

Do not promise a credit or issue a refund based only on the current seat count. If the charge still appears incorrect, document the period and evidence and route a refund or billing adjustment request through the approval process. Any consequential billing action requires recorded human approval.
